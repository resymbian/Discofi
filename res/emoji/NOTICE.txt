contributors, licensed under CC-BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were taken from the Discord J2ME project at commit
7468c90afece547e75de90a82f2ca8bd8ea640da
(https://github.com/gtrxAC/discord-j2me, MIT licensed client code).

emoji.dat is a compact conversion of that project's emoji.json and
emoji_proxyless.json, keeping the same 544-entry / 45-cell-per-sheet layout.
Its SHA-256 is recorded in res/emoji.sha256 and checked by sdk/package.py.
